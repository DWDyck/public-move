# PUBLIC / MOVE St. Louis: $20M for 100 autonomous cars

**Will the money come back?** It can, if each car averages about 14 or more paid rides a day, payers buy rides at about $19, and Missouri allows driverless service. This is a cost-recovery fleet, not a profit machine.

## The deal

- **Ask:** $20M as a recoverable investment (loan or program-related investment), repaid from ride revenue.
- **Security:** the 100 cars, held by a public-benefit trust and resaleable.
- **Staged:** $0.5M now for law, operator terms and community design. $19.5M only after Missouri allows driverless service, an operator signs and insurance is in place.

## Does it pay back? (projection, 5 years)

| Rides per car per day | Annual surplus | Share of $20M back after 5 yrs |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | $0.3M | 29% |
| 12 | $2.3M | 80% |
| **16** | **$4.4M** | **131%** |
| 20 | $6.4M | 182% |

Break-even is about **14 rides per car per day**. Waymo averages about 23 in mature cities; St. Louis has no track record, so 12–16 is the planning range.

## Why payers buy rides

| | Cost per ride |
|---|---|
| This fleet (planning figure) | **$19** |
| Waymo average fare | $19.69 |
| ADA paratransit, U.S. agency average | $40–50 (rider pays $2) |

If 1 in 5 rides replaces a $45 trip, the public saves about **$2.3M a year**.

## Feasibility: what must be true

1. **Law.** Missouri's driverless bill (HB 2069) failed in May 2026. Waymo is only test-driving with safety drivers in St. Louis.
2. **Ownership.** Waymo owns and runs its own cars. A third-party owner needs a negotiated deal or another operator. A **per-ride purchase agreement** (no car purchase) may be the faster first step.
3. **Demand.** Rider fares under the $18 monthly cap bring in only about $1M a year, roughly 12% of the revenue needed. Employers, health plans and city programs must buy the rest.
4. **Access.** Ramp-equipped autonomous vehicles are not yet generally available; bridge with partner ramp vans.

## Model (planning assumptions, replace with operator quotes)

- $17.5M for 100 cars at $175K (industry estimate, $150–200K). $2.5M for depot, accessible fit-out and foundation.
- 5-year life, 25% resale value. $19 average net per ride.
- Operating cost: $55K per car-year at 12 rides a day, scaling with use, plus $0.5M a year for app and admin.

## Next step

Fund the $0.5M foundation: get an operator term sheet and a state-law path. If either fails, the $19.5M is never spent.
